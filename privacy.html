Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

Overview

Start Forge Academy provides business direction consulting. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit this website, book a call, contact me, purchase services, or work with me.

Information I Collect

I may collect information you choose to provide, including:

  • Name, email address, and contact details.
  • Scheduling details submitted through Calendly.
  • Payment and billing details processed through Stripe.
  • Information you share in forms, worksheets, calls, emails, or consulting sessions.
  • Business ideas, goals, constraints, work history, skills, and other planning details.

How I Use Information

I use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule calls.
  • Provide consulting services and prepare client materials.
  • Process payments and manage purchases.
  • Send service-related messages and follow-ups.
  • Improve the website, offers, and client experience.

Third-Party Services

This website and the services may use third-party providers, including Calendly for scheduling and Stripe for payment processing. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies and security practices.

Payment Information

Payment details are handled by Stripe. I do not intentionally collect or store full card numbers on this website.

Client Confidentiality

I treat client planning materials, business ideas, worksheets, and strategy conversations as confidential. I do not sell client information. I may share information only when needed to provide services, comply with law, process payment, protect rights or safety, or with your permission.

Data Retention

I keep information for as long as reasonably needed to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and improve the business.

Your Choices

You can ask to access, correct, or delete information you have provided, subject to legal, accounting, security, and legitimate business recordkeeping needs.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Start Forge Academy at hello@startforge.academy.
